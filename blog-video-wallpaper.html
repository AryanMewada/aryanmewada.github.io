Why Do Most Wallpaper Engines Cause Game Lag?

To understand the solution, you need to understand the problem. A video file (like MP4 or MKV) is heavily compressed. To play that video on your screen, your computer has to constantly "decode" it in real-time. Software like Wallpaper Engine forces your CPU or GPU to decode this video 60 times a second, non-stop, right on your desktop background.

If you have a high-end RTX graphics card, you might not notice this. But on an older Intel Core i3 or a laptop with integrated graphics, dedicating 20% of your CPU just to play a background video is a disaster. When you launch a heavy game like Valorant, CS:GO, or Minecraft, your computer struggles to run both the game and the wallpaper at the same time, leading to massive stuttering and FPS drops.
