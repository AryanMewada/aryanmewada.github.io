How to Make Windows 10/11 Look Like a Mac (Zero Lag Aesthetic Setup)

By Aryan Mewada • Updated: March 2026

Let's be honest, Apple's macOS looks incredibly clean and aesthetic, but buying a Mac just for the looks is extremely expensive. Fortunately, you can transform your boring Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC into a stunning, minimalist Mac-like masterpiece.

The biggest problem with PC customization? Lag. Most tutorials tell you to download heavy software like Rainmeter or standard Wallpaper Engines that consume 30% of your CPU, making your PC useless for gaming. Today, we will build a beautiful setup using ultra-lightweight tools that keep your PC running at 100% performance.

Step 1: Clean Your Desktop & Center the Taskbar

The first rule of an aesthetic setup is minimalism. You need to hide the clutter.

  • Hide Desktop Icons: Right-click on your desktop -> View -> Uncheck "Show desktop icons".
  • Center Taskbar (Windows 10): Right-click the taskbar, unlock it. Add a "Links" toolbar, push it all the way to the left to force your icons to the center, then lock it again. (Windows 11 does this by default).
  • Hide Search Bar: Right-click Taskbar -> Search -> Hidden.

Step 3: Get the Mac OS Dock

To truly mimic a Mac, you need that floating dock at the bottom of your screen.

You can use a free tool like Nexus Dock or MyDockFinder. Once installed, simply go to its settings, change the theme to "macOS Catalina" or "Big Sur", and drag your favorite apps into it. Combine this by setting your Windows taskbar to "Auto-hide" in the Windows settings, and you will have a perfect Mac look!
