Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

At Aryan Mewada Studios (accessible from https://aryanmewada.github.io), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information (such as your name, email, or phone number) when you download our software (AnyVidWall, AnyWebWall). Our software runs locally on your PC.

2. Local Data Usage

Our applications (.exe files) utilize your computer's local resources (RAM/CPU) to function. They do not upload your personal files, wallpapers, or usage data to any cloud server.

3. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other websites (e.g., GitHub, YouTube). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit.

4. Cookies

Our website does not use cookies to track your personal browsing history. We focus purely on providing lightweight software downloads.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via our GitHub profile or official YouTube channel.
