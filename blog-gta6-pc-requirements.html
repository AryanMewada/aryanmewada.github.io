Can I Run GTA 6 on a Low End PC? Ultimate FPS Boost Guide

By Aryan Mewada • Updated: March 2026

The gaming world is currently obsessing over one massive question: "Will GTA 6 run on my PC?" If you are gaming on a laptop or a low-end desktop with 4GB or 8GB of RAM, you might be worried about massive frame drops, overheating, and unbearable lag.

Rockstar Games has pushed the boundaries of modern graphics, meaning GTA 6 is going to be incredibly demanding. But don't lose hope! With the right optimization tricks and by using smart, lightweight software, you can squeeze every single frame out of your potato PC.

Expected GTA 6 System Requirements

While playing on ultra settings requires a beast of a machine, getting the game to simply run on low settings is a different story. Here is what you realistically need to start the game:

  • OS: Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
  • Processor: Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 (Minimum)
  • RAM: 8GB RAM (4GB is highly risky, but possible with extreme tweaks)
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GTX 1650 or AMD equivalent
  • Storage: 150GB+ SSD (HDD will cause severe texture loading lag)

3 Pro Hacks to Run Heavy Games on a Potato PC

If your PC is hovering around the minimum specs, you cannot afford to have background apps eating your RAM and CPU. Here is how you prepare your PC for heavy games:

1. Debloat Your Windows

Windows 10 and 11 come with dozens of useless background apps (telemetry, Cortana, background updaters). Go to your Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc), navigate to the 'Startup' tab, and disable everything except your graphics drivers and Windows Security.

2. Never Use Heavy Screen Recorders (OBS / Bandicam)

Many gamers want to record their GTA gameplay for YouTube or friends. If you have a low-end PC, using OBS Studio will instantly drop your game by 20 to 30 FPS because it heavily taxes your CPU.

3. Beware of Live Wallpapers

Apps like Wallpaper Engine look cool, but they constantly run 3D rendering in the background, stealing precious VRAM from your graphics card. If you launch GTA 6 while a heavy wallpaper is running, your game will stutter.

Want a cool GTA 6 live wallpaper without the lag? Use AnyVidWall or AnyWebWall. These apps feature a "Smart Gaming Mode" that automatically pauses the wallpaper and frees up 100% of the RAM the moment you launch a full-screen game.

Final Thoughts

Running next-gen games like GTA 6 on older hardware is all about resource management. By keeping your Windows clean, upgrading from an HDD to an SSD, and swapping out heavy software for lightweight alternatives built by Aryan Mewada Studios, you can ensure a smooth, lag-free gaming experience.
